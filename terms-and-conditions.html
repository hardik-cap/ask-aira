Definitions

aiRA
means Capillary's agentic AI platform for brands, made available to Customer.
Credits

1. Credit consumption

  • Credit consumption is defined within the aiRA platform based on the nature of each interaction (including but not limited to analytics queries, configuration actions, and optimization outputs) and may evolve as features expand.
  • All Authorized Users accessing aiRA under Customer's account will consume Credits from Customer's allocated Credit Pool.
  • Credits are allocated at the Customer account level and may be shared across Authorized Users.
  • Credit consumption may vary based on the aiRA feature used, number of prompts, retrieval volume and other processing factors.
  • The Customer is responsible for managing usage by its Authorized Users.
  • The Customer will have access to an aiRA dashboard to track Credit consumption.
  • Capillary's aiRA usage records will be the basis for calculating Credit consumption and overages.

2. Credit validity, expiry, and rollover

  • Credits are valid only during the term specified in the Sales Order or Addendum.
  • Unused Credits will expire at the end of the applicable term and will not be refundable, exchangeable, transferable, or convertible into cash or other services.

3. Overage

  • If Customer's usage exceeds the Credit Pool specified, Capillary will charge Customer for such excess usage at the overage rates specified in the Sales Order or Addendum.
