About Event

No waiting on an analyst. No SQL queue. No “let me pull that and get back to you.”

Join us after work in Chicago for a hands-on session on how AI is changing the way marketing, CRM, analytics, and ecommerce teams work with customer data. This isn’t another panel about the future of AI. You’ll sit down with a real customer analytics problem and use aiRA, our on-demand insights and analytics agent, to explore the data, surface patterns, and turn what you find into decisions your team can act on.

What we’ll dig into

  • Going from a data question to an actionable finding in minutes, not days
  • Uncovering customer segments and behavioral patterns using plain language
  • Investigating the questions that matter most: engagement, redemption, retention, and growth
  • Making AI work alongside the analytics tools and workflows you already rely on
  • Closing the gap between “that’s an interesting insight” and “here’s what we’re doing about it”

How the evening works

We’ll tackle a practical customer analytics challenge together through guided exercises, live demos, and plenty of hands-on exploration. The working session runs from 5:00 to 7:00 PM. Then we close the laptops and head into dinner and drinks, where the best conversations usually happen. Bring a colleague and stay for both.

You’ll walk away with a clear picture of what changes when AI becomes part of your everyday analytics workflow, instead of one more tool sitting on the sidelines.

Who should come

Operators who turn customer signals into campaigns, across Marketing · CRM · Growth · Analytics & BI · Data · Ecommerce

Seats are intentionally limited so everyone gets room to participate, experiment, and ask the hard questions.
