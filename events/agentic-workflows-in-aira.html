About the talk

On Day 2 of Captivate 2026 in Nashville, Harsh Deo and Jack Ready took the stage with one line of travel: from tasks to agentic workflows. It started with what loyalty teams are already building for themselves with aiRA, Capillary’s AI agent. It ended with where aiRA is headed next: running a whole job end to end, across your teams and systems, with your approvals built in.

It starts with apps

The first step is already here. With aiRA, teams describe what they want in the words they’d use with a colleague. No form, no spec, no ticket. What comes back is a working app or dashboard their team can open.

Between July 3 and September 20, 2026, teams built about 500 apps and dashboards, with around 270 shared and live. 59% were built by marketers, not developers.

Next: agentic workflows

An app is the small version of the idea. You still open it, and it does one thing the same way every time. Agentic workflows apply the same idea to work that doesn’t fit on a screen: one instruction, the whole sequence, with your approval inside it.
