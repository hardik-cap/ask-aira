About Event

Presented by

This room is for forward-thinking growth teams who want to level up their game. Who want to keep their customers coming back for more, and win-back the ones who don’t.

In this workshop, product leaders from Capillary are going into the weeds of retention, revenue, and growth. They’ll take you through how the best consumer brands are identifying and converting customer signals into revenue.

What to expect

  • Practical use cases across churn prevention, cross-sell, upsell, repeat purchase and win-back
  • Live AI workflows-from customer signals to activated campaigns in minutes
  • Seamless activation into platforms like Braze, Klaviyo, Snowflake, Databricks and more
  • AI-powered audience discovery and prioritization

You’ll leave with new campaign ideas and use cases you can apply to your own customer base right away.

And a network of sharp growth enthusiasts driving best practices in their organizations.
