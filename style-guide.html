Phase 1, direction

aiRA visual language.

The system behind the site: tokens, type, buttons, cards, navigation and the three motifs that carry aiRA's identity. Every value here is a custom property defined in style/tokens.css. Nothing on a page hardcodes a colour.

Colour

The brand anchor is #526CF6, sampled from the aiRA logo mark. The mark runs a horizontal gradient from #6369F6 to #436FF6 and centres here. This supersedes the violet product pill in the 2026 Capillary brand guide, which predates the current logo. Neutrals are cool and hue-aligned to the indigo so greys never read warm against it.

Typography

Google Sans Flex for every heading, Inter for everything readable (body sits at weight 450 so 14px text does not run thin). Geist Mono carries labels, credits and question strings. Display sizes are fluid; body sizes are fixed so line length stays predictable.

The signature

Three motifs, all derived from the product itself and from the logo's four-point sparkle. None of them is the dashed double-rail system from the base export; that is another product's identity.

1. The sparkle rule

Sections are separated by a mark, not a border. The terminus is asymmetric, so the page reads left to right and has a direction.

2. The thread

The reasoning trace made visual: a sequence of steps, each anchored by a sparkle node. The first node is brand-coloured, the rest recede, so a sequence has a beginning.

Step 01

Read where the data sits

18 months of purchase and loyalty history, in place. Zero copy, no migration.

Step 02

Apply your definitions

Your active-member rule, your tiers, layered on top of the industry playbook.

Step 03

Build the move

A segment and a journey, built and waiting. A person approves before it goes live.

3. The question string

Questions are the product's surface, so they are a first-class typographic object rather than body copy. Mono, quoted by a brand left mark.
