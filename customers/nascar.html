NASCAR’s product marketing team uses aiRA to get answers without waiting on an analyst, to spot shifts in member interest, to report to partners faster and to build promotions in minutes.

The answer sat behind an analyst or a dashboard

Donald Baal runs product marketing at NASCAR. His team markets the sport’s digital products and drives engagement across them, which means a steady run of questions about what members are doing and why.

Those answers used to take a while. A question went to a data analyst, or into a Tableau dashboard, and the team waited. That wait mattered more than usual, because NASCAR runs a great many of its campaigns with its official partners and sponsors, and those partners want answers too.

A conversation, not a queue

With aiRA the team asks in plain language and gets the answer in the same sitting. It watches for shifts as well: when members start to lose interest in a promotion, or interest in one starts to climb, aiRA points it out. And when the answer has to travel upward, it builds the report and the executive dashboard that carry it.

It learned the sport, not just the data

What surprised the team was how quickly aiRA picked up NASCAR itself. Compare a week this season with the same week last year and it does not stop at the week number: it accounts for the race having aired on a different network with a smaller audience, so the comparison is like for like and the gap in engagement is explained rather than misread.

It reads signals across the sport as well as the programme, and it gets sharper the more the team uses it.

Partners and promotions, in minutes

Two jobs moved most. Partner questions get answered in real time, with ideas for how to improve a campaign rather than only a report on how it did. And the team has started building promotions with aiRA as well, and turns changes around in minutes.
