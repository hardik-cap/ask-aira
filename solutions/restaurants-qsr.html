Why it is different here

Marketing in QSR is not marketing in retail.

The frequency is daily, the menu changes monthly, and half the P&Ls belong to franchisees. Three things aiRA is built around here.

The visit is a habit

Lapse shows in weeks, not quarters, so the win-back has to move at the same speed. aiRA flags the weekly regular who went quiet at thirty days, with the journey already drafted.

Dayparts are different customers

The breakfast regular and the Friday dinner family share a loyalty card and nothing else. Menu drops, offers and locations all read by daypart, not just by week.

One brand, many P&Ls

Franchisees need answers about their own trade area without a central analyst queue. Any pull becomes a report a location can read itself.
