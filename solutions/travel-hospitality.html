Why it is different here

The year has two bookings in it.

Daily-frequency playbooks fail here. Three things aiRA is built around when the purchase cycle is measured in seasons.

Loyalty has to survive the silence

Months of no contact is the normal state, not a churn signal. Lapse is read against booking windows and seasons, so the win-back lands when the member is deciding, not when a timer fires.

One booking carries the quarter

With tickets this size, a mispriced offer is expensive. Every proposed campaign shows its reasoning and waits for approval before anything sends.

Status is the product

The member one stay short of keeping status is your most persuadable customer, and the easiest to lose. aiRA keeps that segment current without anyone asking for it.
