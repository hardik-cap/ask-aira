The shape of it

An enthusiast and a gift buyer, through the same door.

Four things about specialty data that make a generic analytics answer the wrong answer.

Frequency

Bunched into a short season. A customer can buy four times inside one season and nothing for the next nine months, so a rolling twelve-month average describes a person who does not exist.

Margin

Healthy per item and slow to release. The range depth that earns the customer's trust is the same range depth that holds capital on a shelf until the season comes round again.

Seasonality

Short and unforgiving. A season that opens two weeks late compresses the entire year, and the gifting peak brings a completely different buyer through the same door.

What the data misses

Who the product is for. Gifting breaks the link between the buyer and the user, and club, team or trade purchases sit under one account that behaves like nobody in particular.
