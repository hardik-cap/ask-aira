The shape of it

One shopper, many missions, no single owner.

Four things about department and discount data that make a generic analytics answer the wrong answer.

Frequency

Regular, but never the same trip twice. One customer arrives for a birthday present, a school uniform and a set of towels, and each visit reads like a different person in the data.

Margin

Thin and highly mixed. Margin varies more between your own departments than between you and a competitor, so a growing basket can quietly be a shrinking contribution.

Seasonality

Concentrated. A large share of the year lands in a handful of weeks, and the rest of the calendar is judged mostly on whether it set those weeks up.

What the data misses

The shopper across departments. Buying is organised by department and so is the reporting, so the customer who crosses is the one no report is built to find and no budget is built to win.
