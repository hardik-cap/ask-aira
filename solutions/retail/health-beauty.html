The shape of it

Routines, not seasons. Rules, not preferences.

Four things about health and beauty data that make a generic analytics answer the wrong answer.

Frequency

Set by the product rather than the mood. Replenishment cycles are countable, so the next purchase has a date on it and a late one is a dated event rather than a rolling average.

Margin

Two businesses under one roof. Beauty carries the margin and moves on newness; the dispensary and everyday health lines are regulated, thin and not up for negotiation.

Seasonality

Regimens rather than quarters. Demand moves when a customer's routine changes, and that happens on their calendar, not the trading one.

What the data misses

What you are not permitted to use. This is the most restricted customer data in retail, so the boundary has to hold in the tooling rather than in a policy document, on every single query.
