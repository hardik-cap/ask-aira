The shape of it

Out of market for years, then urgent for a fortnight.

Four things about home and furniture data that make a generic analytics answer the wrong answer.

Frequency

Years, not months. Most of the file is out of market on any given day, and the few who are in market look identical to everyone else until roughly a week before they buy.

Margin

High ticket, eaten from both ends. Delivery, assembly, bulky returns and financing all sit between the price on the tag and what actually lands.

Seasonality

Tied to life events more than to the trading calendar: moving, renovating, a new arrival. The peaks are real, but the trigger is personal and invisible in the sales data.

What the data misses

The project. One renovation shows up as a series of unrelated orders across months, channels and sometimes two names, so the most valuable unit of demand is the one nothing in the stack records.
