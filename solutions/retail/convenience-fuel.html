The shape of it

Two minutes, twice a week, every week.

Four things about forecourt data that make a generic analytics answer the wrong answer.

Frequency

The highest in retail, attached to the smallest baskets in retail. Several visits a week, a couple of items each, and the whole decision made in under two minutes.

Margin

Fuel moves the volume and the shop makes the money. Ranking customers by spend puts the least profitable ones at the top, which is why fuel volume is a misleading loyalty metric.

Seasonality

The clock matters more than the calendar. Morning coffee, lunch, the evening top-up and the weekend trip are four different businesses running through one site.

What the data misses

The link between the pump and the till. Fuel-only transactions are often anonymous, so the same person can appear as a loyal member inside the shop and a stranger on the forecourt.
