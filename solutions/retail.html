Eight kinds of retail

Grocery is not fashion. Fuel is not furniture.

A weekly shop and a sofa bought once a decade are different businesses wearing the same word. Pick the one that is yours.

Grocery & Supermarket Weekly trips, thin margins. Small basket and frequency shifts move the whole number. Fashion & Apparel Drops and seasons spike demand, returns eat margin. Repeat buyers decide the year. Specialty & Outdoor Retail Big-ticket enthusiasts who shop in seasons. Who returns between peaks matters most. Home & Furniture High tickets, years between purchases. The work is staying relevant in the gap. Convenience & Fuel One customer, two datasets: forecourt and store. The margin lives inside the shop. Health & Beauty Replenishment you can set a clock by. A missed refill window is churn seen early. Department & Discount Wide assortments, thin margins. Cross-category movement decides who becomes loyal. Other Retail Electronics, marketplaces and D2C. Different catalogues, same need: answers fast.

Asked in retail

One question, taken all the way to a decision.

Every answer arrives with the reasoning shown, earns the next question, and ends as a move built and waiting on a person.

Which lapsed buyers are worth winning back before the festive window?

The answer

A ranked list, not a chart: who lapsed, what they used to spend, and the margin each name carried through last festive season.

The drill-down

Keep the full-price buyers only. What is the list worth if one in five returns?

The move

A win-back journey for the shortlist, budget and send window already set.

Which online customers have never been matched to a store visit?

The answer

The unmatched cohort, split by distance to the nearest store and by what they already buy online.

The drill-down

Hold everyone inside a ten-minute drive. Which stores stand to gain the most?

The move

A store-introduction offer for the near-store cohort, scoped store by store.

Which customers have dropped a department they used to buy every season?

The answer

Who dropped it, whether they moved to another department or lapsed outright, and what the gap costs a season.

The drill-down

Take the ones still loyal elsewhere. What would bring the department back for them?

The move

A category nudge timed to the season opening, one per customer, not a blast.
